# Annual leave
Full-time employees at Northgate Labs get 27 days of annual leave plus UK bank holidays. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. You can carry over up to 5 unused days, which must be used by 31 March.

# Sick leave
Tell your manager before 9:30am on your first day of absence. A fit note from a doctor is needed after 7 calendar days. Company sick pay covers 20 working days at full pay in any 12 months.

# Parental leave
Birthing parents get 26 weeks at full pay. Partners get 6 weeks at full pay, which can be split into two blocks.
