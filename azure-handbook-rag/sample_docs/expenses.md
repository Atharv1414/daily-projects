# Travel expenses
Book standard-class rail travel. Hotels are capped at £160 per night in London and £110 elsewhere. Meals while travelling are capped at £35 per day.

# Claiming expenses
Submit claims with receipts within 60 days. Claims approved by the 20th are paid in that month's payroll. Any single claim over £500 needs director approval.

# Home office budget
New starters can spend up to £400 on home office equipment during their first 90 days.
